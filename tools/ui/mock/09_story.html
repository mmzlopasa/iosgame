Chapter 1 · Stone Age
The Riverside Camp
The winter in the north was the hardest anyone could remember. When the reindeer did not return, the families followed the river south, carrying fire in a clay pot.
After many days they came to a valley none of them had seen: a quiet river, a great lake beyond the trees and hills of grey stone.
Survive the first year and make the camp a stable village.
